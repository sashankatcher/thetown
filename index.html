We welcome you to frogmouth

Where culture and nature live in harmony.

Established in 1732, Frogmouth was founded to be the center for development of new technologies. What started as primarily agricultural research would later expand as interest in the surrounding wildlife grew.

Over the centuries as the population increased, frogmouth would gain a reputation for its stunning vistas, fabulous wildlife and expand into the premier sightseeing destination of the western United States of America. Our town council works hard to keep up the image of frogmouth as the definitive small western american town.

Research and development continues as we are determined to reshape the future of this planet for the benefit of all.
